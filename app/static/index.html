What this is

      
A ledger for business ideas. You say what to look at; candidates
      are generated, gated against your rules, checked for real cited
      competitors, then scored. Gates and scoring are plain code — no model assigns a
      number — so the same evidence always gives the same score.


      
Just talk to it

      
There are no commands to learn. Type what you want:

      
lets look at custom wooden furniture in Florida
why was that one rejected?
what is that score resting on?
add a rule rejecting anything needing more than 8 hours a week
how does the scoring work here?


      
What the outcomes mean

      

        
scored
            Passed your gates, found enough cited competitors, got a number.

        
gate rejected
            Killed by one of your rules, in code, before any model judged it.
            The rule is named.

        
under researched
            Not a failure. Too few competitors with resolvable urls,
            so it refused to score rather than score thin evidence.

        
refuted
            A check found the premise does not hold.

        
duplicate
            Already on your reject list. Reviving one is a deliberate act.

      


      
The tabs

      

        
Output
            The selected idea in full: every dimension with its value,
            confidence, provenance and falsifier. Override a value here, or archive it.

        
Ideas
            Everything in the ledger with status and score. Click to open.

        
Rules
            Your gates. Add one below the list, or just ask in chat —
            either way it is previewed against the ledger before it can be activated.

        
Facts
            Editable. Gates read these by name, so this is where you
            change what gets rejected without touching a rule.

        
Setup
            Provider, key, model. Several keys can be saved and named.

        
Check
            Ask the app to diagnose itself. Start here when something
            looks wrong.

      


      
Reading a score

      
The column that matters is what would change it — the
      falsifier. It names the observation that would move that number. If you cannot
      imagine checking it, the score is decoration. Provenance says whether a
      value was derived from evidence or set by you; overridden values are excluded
      from calibration, so a judgement call never teaches the system it was a
      measurement.


      
Why a run might say demo

      
Candidates named demo- are fictional. That happens
      when the provider cannot search the web, because the alternative is letting a
      model invent competitors. The gates and scoring still ran for real. The
      Check tab names the exact cause.


      
Where your data is

      
Everything is on this machine. The key is in your OS secret
      store, never in the repository or the config file. Archives are dated folders
      under your ledger directory containing dossier.md and
      snapshot.json — plain files, readable without this app.
