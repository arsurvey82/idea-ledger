What this is

        
A ledger that keeps scores honest as facts change. It proposes ideas, but its
        real job is revision.

        
Provenance

        
Every number is derived (computed from evidence), overridden
        (you edited it; the original is kept), or seeded (imported, no evidence).
        Overrides are excluded from calibration — counting your corrections as model
        error would make the system measure you instead of it.

        
Tracks

        
Scores rank within a track only. A physical-goods idea and a service idea are
        not on one scale, so the system refuses to sort them together.

        
Under-researched

        
Not a low score — a terminal state. If too few citable competitors were
        found, the idea is not scored at all rather than scored on thin evidence.

        
Breaking changes

        
Adding, removing or redefining a dimension means every stored score stops
        being comparable to every future one, and the missing evidence cannot honestly
        be backfilled. The ledger versions rather than pretends.

        
Your data

        
Facts, ideas and evidence live outside this repository and are never
        committed. The framework ships; your profile does not.
